Why a blog matters for SEO

A blog gives a store fresh, keyword-relevant content to target long-tail search queries that product pages can't — questions like "how to choose a laptop backpack" or "best yoga mat for beginners." It also creates natural internal linking opportunities back to category and product pages, which is exactly what the Shop page and product pages link back to here.

In a real project, each card above would link to its own full article page. In this training build, they're kept as excerpts so students can focus on list-page SEO (structured post previews, unique meta description, category taxonomy) before building out full article templates as a practice exercise — see ASSIGNMENTS.md.
