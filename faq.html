Answers about our anti-tarnish jewellery

What is anti-tarnish jewellery?

Anti-tarnish jewellery is made or finished to help reduce the dulling or colour change that can happen over time. Materials, finish and everyday care all affect how a piece looks with regular wear.

Is Zevorelle jewellery water-resistant?

Zevorelle focuses on water-resistant jewellery designed for everyday wear. To help your pieces last longer, dry them after contact with water and remove them before swimming, showering or using household cleaners.

How should I care for anti-tarnish jewellery?

Apply perfume, lotion and hair products before putting on jewellery. Wipe each piece with a soft dry cloth after wearing it, keep it dry and store it separately in a pouch or jewellery box to help prevent scratches and tangles.

Is Zevorelle jewellery skin-friendly?

Our collection includes skin-friendly and nickel-free styles. As sensitivities differ from person to person, please stop wearing any piece that feels uncomfortable and contact us if you need help choosing a style.

What types of jewellery does Zevorelle offer?

Zevorelle offers anti-tarnish rings, necklaces, earrings, bracelets and anklets. Explore the collection to find everyday pieces, easy-to-layer styles and gift-worthy designs.

How can I order from Zevorelle?

Browse the Zevorelle collection and select Add to Cart on a style you love. Contact us by phone, WhatsApp or email to confirm availability, delivery details and ordering support.

Can I choose Zevorelle jewellery as a gift?

Yes. Zevorelle jewellery is designed for everyday style and thoughtful gifting. If you need help choosing a piece for a birthday, milestone or special occasion, contact our team for guidance.
