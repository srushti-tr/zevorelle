Privacy Policy

How Zevorelle handles information shared through our website and customer support channels.

Home › Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 July 2026

Zevorelle respects your privacy. This policy explains the information we may receive when you browse our website or contact us, why we use it, and the choices available to you. If you have questions, email zevorelle@gmail.com or call/WhatsApp +91 87084 61549.

1. Who we are

In this policy, “Zevorelle”, “we”, “us” and “our” refer to Zevorelle, a jewellery business based in Rewari, Haryana, India. Our website is available at zevorelle.netlify.app.

2. Information we may collect

The information we collect depends on how you interact with us. It may include:

  • your name, phone number, email address and message when you contact us;
  • order, delivery or product-preference details you choose to share while seeking support or placing an order;
  • communications you send through email, phone or WhatsApp; and
  • limited device-side information required for site features, such as the number of items saved in your cart.

Please do not send sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for your request. Zevorelle does not ask you to send card, bank-account, UPI PIN or password details by email or WhatsApp.

3. How we use information

We may use your information to respond to enquiries, help with product selection, process and support an order, arrange delivery where applicable, communicate service updates, prevent misuse, and improve our customer experience. We use information only for purposes connected with your request or our legitimate business operations, subject to applicable law.

4. Payments and third-party services

This website does not host a payment-card form. If you choose to make a payment through a payment provider or other service approved for your order, that provider may process information under its own privacy notice and security practices. We do not receive or store your full payment-card details through this website.

5. Cookies and local storage

Our current website uses browser local storage to remember the item count shown in the cart on your device. This information remains in your browser until you clear it. If analytics, advertising cookies or other third-party tools are introduced later, this policy will be updated to describe them and, where required, provide appropriate choices.

6. When we share information

We do not sell personal information. We may share the minimum information needed with service providers that help us fulfil a customer request, such as a delivery partner or payment provider, and where disclosure is required by law or necessary to protect rights, safety or security.

7. Keeping information secure

We take reasonable steps to protect information from unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure. No online service can guarantee absolute security, so please use trusted channels and contact us promptly if you believe your information has been misused.

8. Your choices and requests

You may ask us to update, correct or delete information we hold about you, or withdraw a marketing preference, by contacting us at zevorelle@gmail.com. We may need to keep certain records where required for legal, accounting, fraud-prevention or order-support purposes. We will handle requests in accordance with applicable law.

9. External links

Our website may link to third-party services, including WhatsApp. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, and we encourage you to review them before sharing information or making a payment.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our website, services or legal obligations change. The “Last updated” date at the top shows when it was most recently revised. Continued use of the website after a revised policy is published means you acknowledge the updated version.

11. Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, contact Zevorelle at zevorelle@gmail.com, call/WhatsApp +91 87084 61549, or visit our Contact page. Our support hours are 9:00 AM to 9:00 PM IST.
