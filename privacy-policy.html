Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 July 2026

Home › Privacy Policy

This is a demo policy for a training project. Express Store By Kaushaindra is not a real business, does not process real orders or payments, and does not knowingly collect personal data for any commercial purpose. This page exists to show students how a Privacy Policy page should be structured for SEO and legal completeness.

1. Information we would collect

In a real store, this section would list data such as name, email address, shipping address and payment details collected through account creation, checkout and newsletter sign-up forms. On this demo site, the contact and newsletter forms do not transmit data anywhere — see js/main.js.

2. How information would be used

  • To process and deliver orders
  • To respond to customer service enquiries
  • To send optional marketing emails (only with consent)
  • To improve the website experience

3. Cookies and local storage

This site uses localStorage only to remember a demo cart item count on your device. No cookies, analytics or third-party trackers are installed by default.

4. Third-party services

Product images are served from a placeholder image service, and fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. No other third-party scripts run on this site.

5. Your rights

In a production store, this section would explain how users can access, correct or delete their personal data, in line with applicable law such as India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act.

6. Contact

Questions about this demo policy can be sent via the Contact page.
